# HY4 Flash tuning on November 23, 2026

One DGX Spark ran the pinned AngelSlim Spark-4Bit-Fast GGUF over the loopback API. The single-user comparisons held the 196,608-token configured context, F16 KV, Engram placement, sampling settings and 400-token thinking-on prose workload fixed. Each startup was followed by an unscored warmup and six measured requests.

## Startup

The Direct I/O arm changed only `--load-mode` from `mmap` to `dio`. The lazy CPU Engram mapping stayed enabled. Launch-to-health was measured in whole seconds. The decode results follow a warmup request, and the filesystem page cache was left in its normal state.

| Profile | Launch to health | Median decode | Median TTFT |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| [mmap control A](../results/tuning-20261123/mmap-control-a.json) | 175 s | 29.60 tok/s | 135 ms |
| [Direct I/O](../results/tuning-20261123/direct-io.json) | 12 s | 27.94 tok/s | 135 ms |
| [mmap control B](../results/tuning-20261123/mmap-control-b.json) | 161 s | 28.10 tok/s | 134 ms |

Direct I/O is available via `HY4_LOAD_MODE=dio`. mmap remains the default because this experiment measured a startup gain and did not establish an improved decode rate. The scored Direct I/O launcher passed `--load-mode dio` and produced no buffered-fallback warning. An earlier trial labeled Direct I/O actually used mmap, so it was excluded and rerun. ## Single-user decode

All profiles below used mmap. A candidate had to improve median decode by more than 3% against both neighboring controls, with no more than 3% median TTFT regression against either.

MTP ×2 was 10.4–10.8% faster than its adjacent controls. MTP ×4 was slower on this prose prompt. Extra CUDA graph scheduling did not clear the threshold against both of its controls. The final control also ran two 32K cold-prefill checks with the existing 1,024-token microbatch. Both retrieved the exact key at 691.49 and 703.11 client tok/s. The larger microbatch test was deferred to leave time for the selected profile’s validation. ## MTP ×2 qualification

A fresh six-request replay reached **30.96 tok/s median**, **32.00 tok/s peak** and **135 ms median TTFT**. Standard chat, separate reasoning, exact Traditional Chinese copying and the tool-call round trip passed. Unique 32K and 128K prompts both returned the exact retrieval code with zero cached input tokens. Recorded client prefill was **695.1 tok/s** at **47.13 s TTFT** and **594.1 tok/s** at **220.61 s TTFT**. Peak sampled GPU-process allocation was **92.7 GiB** with a minimum **22.3 GiB** host `MemAvailable`. The new default is MTP ×2 for one slot.
